# Curio

Platform: web
Kind: Whole-life personal archive, taste graph, and modular MCP memory vault

## Product Truth
- Curio is a single searchable, sortable, and selectively shareable digital vault for a whole life: cultural artifacts (books, films, music, art, recipes, places, quotes), wardrobe & outfits (`Closet`), owned inventory (`My Stuff` / Purchase Hub), encrypted private memos, and future personal finance (`Scrooge`), health/fitness biometrics, and life timelines.
- Exposes a **Modular MCP** layer allowing owners to grant AI agents scoped access to selected domains (`Books`, `Recipes`, etc.) while keeping sensitive vaults (`Closet`, `My Stuff`, `Private Memos`, future Finance/Health) sealed by default.
- Automatic domain visualizations match each medium: Stripe Press 3D hardcovers for `Books`, 3D cinema posters for `Films`, 3D LP vinyl crates for `Music`, 2D editorial grids for `Closet`/`My Stuff`, and natural-ratio masonry for `Stream`, `Recipes`, and `Places`.
- Supports local-first private collections alongside intentionally shared read-only editorial boards (`/curio/list/[slug]`) with authentic source links and synaptic connections (`PAIRED_WITH`, `WORN_ON`).
- Sync pipelines (`src/lib/sync/*`) populate wardrobe/outfits, Purchase Hub inventory, Goodreads books, Letterboxd/FilmAffinity films, and Chef UI recipes.

## Aesthetic & Craft Constraints
- Aesthetic direction: **Clean Gallery Elevation** paired with **Stripe Press Dark Bindery** 3D stages (`iA Writer`, `The Sartorialist`, `Stripe Press`, `Vitsoe 606`).
- Palette: Warm Gallery plaster (`#F2EFE9`) + elevated `#FFFFFF` card stock + **Viridian Library** (`#1B5E46` / `#4CA382`) for human curation; Dark Obsidian (`#121110` / `#1C1A17`); **Prussian Ink / Archival Cobalt** (`#1B3B6F` / `#6B93F5`) reserved exclusively for active MCP agent-scope controls.
- Navigation & hierarchy: 3 crisp tiers (`Top Boards -> Sub-Boards -> Contextual Filters`) under a compact **H1 Framed Folio (`Curio`)** masthead, type-scoped actions, and zero fabricated footer links.
- Mobile (`< 768px`): Single auto-hiding `< 48px` sticky bar paired with `<dialog>` filter/MCP sheets and a `100dvh` horizontal `scroll-snap` Exhibition Deck.
